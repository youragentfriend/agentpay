AGENTPAY GOOGLE AI STUDIO SPEECH CLIPS

SCENE
The AgentPay product demo. A clean, modern four-minute startup presentation for judges and general viewers. One narrator guides the viewer while matching product actions happen on screen. The mood is confident, clear, energetic, and trustworthy.

SAMPLE CONTEXT
Speak in clear international English at a moderately brisk pace. Sound like a confident founder presenting a useful product, not a salesperson reading an advertisement. Use simple words, short sentences, natural emphasis, and brief pauses between screen actions. Keep the delivery warm and energetic, but controlled. Clearly pronounce AgentPay, Binance Pay, Agentic Wallet, and x four-oh-two. Do not sound dramatic, robotic, overly formal, or like an instructional classroom video.

CLIP 00 — OPENING
AgentPay is a universal payment layer built on Binance Agent OS. It brings Binance Pay, wallet transfers, paid online services, tracking, and safety controls into one place—for both people and AI agents.

CLIP 01 — OVERVIEW
The Overview is the main control center. It shows the AgentPay Assistant, recent requests, and the status of every connected payment option.

CLIP 02 — BINANCE PORTFOLIO
The Binance page gives a clear view of balances across Spot, Funding, Futures, Earn, and Margin, so AgentPay understands what funds are available.

CLIP 03 — BINANCE PAY
Binance Pay lets AgentPay inspect and pay supported QR codes or payment links. It can also create an official receive link for requesting money.

CLIP 04 — AGENTIC WALLET
The Agentic Wallet handles direct wallet transfers. It shows live balances, supported networks, receive addresses, and transaction history, with a clear review before every transfer.

CLIP 05 — X402 SERVICES
x four-oh-two Services is for online tools that charge each time they are used. AgentPay can find a service, show the price, and prepare payment before opening it.

CLIP 06 — ACTIVITY AND REPORTS
Activity keeps every payment and status in one searchable list. Reports then turn those records into totals, trends, asset breakdowns, and daily spending.

CLIP 07 — SETTINGS AND CONTROL
Settings keeps payments under control. Users choose approved wallet addresses, spending limits, and payment switches. Connections and Diagnostics show what is working, while General stores display preferences. Every outgoing payment still needs review and approval.

CLIP 08 — PHASE 2 TRANSITION
Now let’s prove that AgentPay is not just a dashboard. These are real payments.

CLIP 09 — INSPECT THE BINANCE PAY QR
I’ll upload a Binance Pay QR code. AgentPay reads the request first and shows the payee and amount, so nothing is paid before the details are checked.

CLIP 10 — CONFIRM THE BINANCE PAY PAYMENT
The request is correct, so I’ll confirm it. AgentPay sends the payment through Binance Pay and records the final result for tracking.

CLIP 11 — GENERATE A RECEIVE LINK
AgentPay can receive money too. I’ll choose USDT, enter a small amount, and create an official Binance Pay link that can be shared with another user.

CLIP 12 — PREPARE THE AGENTIC WALLET TRANSFER
Next, I’ll send USDT through the Agentic Wallet. I choose the asset, network, amount, and recipient. AgentPay checks the balance and shows the exact transfer before money can move.

CLIP 13 — APPROVE AND SEND THE WALLET TRANSFER
The amount, network, and recipient are correct. I approve this exact request, then send it. AgentPay records the transaction ID so the transfer can be checked publicly.

CLIP 14 — UNIFIED ACTIVITY AND REPORTS
Both payments now appear in Activity, even though they used different payment methods. Reports brings them together again, showing where, when, and how AgentPay was used.

CLIP 15 — AUTOMATION TRANSITION
We have shown the payment tools working manually. Next, AgentPay Assistant will turn the same actions into one simple conversation, without removing the checks and approvals.
