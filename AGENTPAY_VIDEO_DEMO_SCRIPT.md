# AgentPay Video Demo Script

**Target final length:** approximately 4 minutes
**This document covers:** Introduction, Phase 1, Phase 2, and the transition to Phase 3
**Expected duration covered here:** approximately 3 minutes
**Reserved for Phase 3 and closing:** approximately 1 minute

## Recording method

1. Generate each numbered narration clip separately in Google AI Studio.
2. Put the audio clips on the editing timeline first.
3. Record each matching screen action as a separate video clip.
4. Remove loading time, file-picker windows, typing mistakes, and long waits.
5. Place the click at the marked cue. Hold completed screens for one or two seconds.
6. Do not record Phase 3 or the final closing yet.

## Google AI Studio speech context

### Scene

> The AgentPay product demo. A clean, modern four-minute startup presentation for judges and general viewers. One narrator guides the viewer while matching product actions happen on screen. The mood is confident, clear, energetic, and trustworthy.

### Sample context

> Speak in clear international English at a moderately brisk pace. Sound like a confident founder presenting a useful product, not a salesperson reading an advertisement. Use simple words, short sentences, natural emphasis, and brief pauses between screen actions. Keep the delivery warm and energetic, but controlled. Clearly pronounce AgentPay, Binance Pay, Agentic Wallet, and x four-oh-two. Do not sound dramatic, robotic, overly formal, or like an instructional classroom video.
